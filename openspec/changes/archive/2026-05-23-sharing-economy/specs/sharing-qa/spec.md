## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Item Q&A
The system SHALL allow authenticated members to ask questions publicly on a published item.

#### Scenario: Asking a question
- **WHEN** a member submits a question on an item
- **THEN** the question is visible on the item page and the owner is notified

### Requirement: Owner Answers
The system SHALL allow the item owner to answer questions asked on their item.

#### Scenario: Answering a question
- **WHEN** the owner submits an answer to a question
- **THEN** the answer is displayed publicly beneath the question and the asking member is notified
