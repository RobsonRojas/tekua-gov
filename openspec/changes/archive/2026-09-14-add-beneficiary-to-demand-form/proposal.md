# Proposal: Allow Selecting a Beneficiary in Demand Form

## What Changes
- Add a new "Beneficiário" selection field to both the demand creation and editing forms.
- The field should allow selecting from available beneficiaries.
- The selected beneficiary must be saved and updated when submitting the form to the backend.

## Why
Currently, the demand form lacks a way to explicitly assign or select a beneficiary for the demand during its creation or editing process. This functionality is essential to properly link tasks to their intended recipients.
